Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application hairLog. We are committed to an absolute "privacy-by-design" approach. Our core philosophy is that your personal data belongs to you, and you alone.

1. 100% Local Storage & No Server Architecture

hairLog operates under a strict **Zero-Server Architecture**. We do not own, manage, or maintain any external cloud servers to collect your information.

  • Absolute Local Storage: All data, including your daily logs, scalp notes, hair growth photos, and treatment history, is stored 100% locally on your iOS device's secure sandbox storage.
  • No Data Transmitted: No personal records are ever transmitted, uploaded, or leaked to the internet. If you delete the app, all your local data will be permanently removed from your device, as we do not keep backups.
  • No Registration: There are no accounts, cloud logins, or user profiles. You can use all features offline.

2. Third-Party Services & Technical Diagnostics

To ensure app stability, we may rely solely on Apple’s native, opt-in crash reporting systems (such as TestFlight or App Store diagnostics). These systems only collect anonymized technical logs (e.g., system version, device model, crash stacks) provided by Apple. They do not contain any personal records or photos.

3. Data Security & User Control

Because your data never leaves your device, its security is fully integrated with iOS security mechanisms. We strongly recommend securing your device with a passcode, Touch ID, or Face ID to prevent unauthorized physical access to your data.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this strictly offline Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us at: happyonex@163.com

隐私政策

更新日期：2026年5月

本隐私政策适用于移动设备应用程序 hairLog。我们坚守“原生隐私（Privacy by Design）”的设计理念。我们的核心原则是：您的个人数据仅属于您自己。

1. 100% 本地存储与无服务器架构

hairLog 采用严格的 **无服务器架构（Zero-Server Architecture）**。我们不拥有、不运营、亦不维护任何外部云端服务器来收集您的信息。

  • 绝对本地存储： 您在应用内记录的所有日志、头皮笔记、生发照片以及记录历史，均 **100% 存储在您 iOS 设备的本地安全沙盒（Sandbox）中**。
  • 绝不上传网络： 您的任何个人记录绝不会被传输、上传或泄露到互联网上。由于我们不保留任何云端备份，如果您卸载该应用，您的所有本地数据将从您的设备中被永久清除。
  • 无需注册登录： 本应用不设账户系统，无需绑定手机、邮箱或进行任何云端登录，所有功能均可完全离线使用。

2. 第三方服务与技术诊断

为了保证应用的稳定性，我们仅依赖苹果官方原生、且需用户授权的崩溃报告系统（如 TestFlight 或 App Store 诊断）。这些系统仅收集由苹果提供的匿名技术日志（如系统版本、设备型号、崩溃堆栈），绝对不包含任何您的个人记录或照片。

3. 数据安全与用户控制

由于您的核心数据从未离开过您的手机，其安全性与 iOS 系统的自身安全机制完全绑定。我们强烈建议您为设备设置锁屏密码、Touch ID 或 Face ID，以防止他人未经授权物理接触您的本地数据。
